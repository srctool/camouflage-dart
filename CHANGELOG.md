## [Unreleased]
- M0: workspace skeleton. A pub workspace managed by Melos with `camouflage_core`, `camouflage_skin_minimal` and `camouflage_skin_testing`; the shared analysis options; the layer and no-Material checks; the example app for all six platforms on `WidgetsApp`; CI.
- M1 to M14: the full v0.1 set. `camouflage_core` (theme with contrast validation, the skin contract, the provider and environment, 52 components), `camouflage_charts`, and five skins from the widgets layer only: Minimal, Fluent (Solid, Acrylic and Mica), Material 3, Cupertino (Classic and glass without refraction) and Carbon.
- M15: tap-target and label guideline checks over every gallery (200% text and right to left); the galleries with controls below the guideline are skipped by name in each skin's accessibility test until fixed.
