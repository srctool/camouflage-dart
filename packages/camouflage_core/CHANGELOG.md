## Unreleased

- Fixed: `CamoPagedList` announced "loading more" with an announcement event, which Android has deprecated (a warning in the log). It now says it through the list's live region, so TalkBack still hears it.
- Skin-drawn scrollbars: `CamoScrollArea(child:)` puts a thin overlay bar over a scrollable, in the skin's colors: a thumb that grows under the pointer, dragged to scroll, a click on the track pages a viewport, and it shows while scrolling (or always, per skin and theme). It reads the scroll position from the child's notifications, so the child needs no controller. It appears automatically in the tree, data table, paged list, menus and the select's list, only with pointer input on desktop and the web; touch platforms keep their native indicators. It takes no focus and is hidden from screen readers; wheel events go to the content. **The skin contract grows by one more required renderer, `scrollbar` (`CamoScrollbarRenderer`)**, so every skin, including your own, must add it (ADR-45).
- Split panes: `CamoSplitPane(axis:, panes:, state:)` lays out two or more panes side by side or stacked with a draggable divider between each pair. The layout is the app's `CamoSplitState` (pane sizes as fractions, so a saved layout restores on any window size, and which panes are collapsed; `snapshot()` and `apply()` to persist it); a `CamoPane` has `minSize` and `maxSize` and can be `collapsible`. A divider is focusable and adjustable by a screen reader ("Resize panel, 35 percent"); arrow keys resize (Shift for a larger step), Home and End go to a limit, Enter collapses or restores, a double tap resets, and right to left mirrors a horizontal split. **The skin contract grows by one renderer, `splitPane` (`CamoSplitPaneRenderer`), so every skin, including your own, must add it** (ADR-45).
- Resizable, hideable and reorderable table columns: pass `columnState: CamoColumnState(ids)` to `CamoDataTable` and the user can drag a header's edge to resize (a double-tap fits the content, using `CamoColumn.text`, or goes back to the column's own width), drag a header or press Alt+Left/Right to reorder, press Ctrl+Left/Right to resize, and right-click a header to hide columns, choose the visible ones or reset the widths. Screen readers get the same as custom actions on the header (Widen, Narrow, Move earlier or later, Hide). The state is the app's (`order`, `widths`, `hidden`, `snapshot()` and `apply()` to save the layout); the last visible column can't be hidden. `CamoColumn` gains `minWidth`, `resizable`, `hideable`, `reorderable` and `text`. Without a `columnState` the table is unchanged; no new renderer.
- Speed search: type in a focused tree, data table or menu to filter it, with the matches highlighted and no search field. `CamoTree(speedSearch: CamoSpeedSearchState())` keeps the ancestors of matches and opens them; `CamoDataTable(speedSearch:)` filters rows by the columns that give their `text`; `CamoMenuAnchor(speedSearch: true)` replaces the type-ahead with a filter. Up and Down move between matches in a tree, Backspace edits, Esc clears before it closes anything, and a live region says "3 matches" or "No matches". `SpeedSearchMode.jump` keeps every item and only moves focus. `CamoMatcher.match(query, text, kind:)` matches by prefix, substring or camel-hump (`fLog` finds `featureLogin`) or fuzzily, ignoring case and accents, and returns the ranges; `CamoHighlightedText(text, ranges)` draws them in `components.speedSearch`'s colors, for your own lists. `TreeNodeSlots.highlight` and `MenuItemState.highlight` carry the ranges to a skin's renderer (the five skins draw them; a third-party skin should draw its label with `CamoHighlightedText`). No new renderer.
- Command palette: `CamoCommandPalette(state:, sources:)` is a popup at the top of the window for "Find action" and "Search everywhere": one field, results grouped by source, keyboard first. Show it from your own shortcut with `CamoCommandPaletteState.show()` (the double-Shift or Ctrl+K trigger is the app's). A `CamoCommandSource(title:, search:)` answers asynchronously; the answer to an older query is dropped, a slow source shows a loading row while the others appear at once, and `CamoCommandSource.of(title:, items:)` scores an in-memory list (ten thousand items filter as you type). Rows show the title with matches highlighted, an icon and a shortcut hint (`CamoCommandItem.shortcut`); with nothing typed each group lists what was run before first (`state.recent`, `snapshot()` to save it). Up and Down move through all rows, Tab and Shift+Tab jump between groups, Enter runs the active row after the palette has closed, Esc clears the query and then closes; focus stays in the field and screen readers hear the active row ("Close File, Actions, 2 of 5"). `CamoMatcher.score(query, text)` ranks a fuzzy match (prefix, then substring, then camel-hump, then scattered letters) and returns `CamoMatch(ranges, score)`. `CamoMenuItemRow` gains `highlight`, `active`, `reserveIcon`, `hint`, `focusable` and `asOption`. `CamoSearchField` now lets Esc go on to its parents when there is nothing to clear. No new renderer: the palette is the skin's popup panel, search field and menu rows.
- Menu submenus: `CamoMenuSubmenu(text:, items:)` is a new `CamoMenuEntry`: a row that opens a nested menu beside it (Reset ▸ Soft, Mixed, Hard). Hover opens it after 250 ms, a tap, Enter and the arrow toward the child open it at once and focus its first item; the arrow back and Esc close only the submenu, and a leaf closes every level. It flips to the start side near the edge and mirrors in right-to-left. `MenuItemState` gains `hasSubmenu` and `submenuOpen`.
